🏆 A Legacy of Service

With 37 years and 10 months of dedicated government service — including a long tenure with U.S. Customs and Border Protection under the Department of Homeland Security — Darren Johnson has shown unwavering commitment to protecting the nation. His leadership and dedication have left a lasting impact on colleagues, communities, and the mission of securing America’s borders.
