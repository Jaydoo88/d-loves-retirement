• Over 1,300 historical horse racing gaming machines
• A full sportsbook lounge
• Multiple dining options
• Full bar and lounge areas

This makes it an ideal place for guests to relax, reconnect, and continue the celebration outside of the main event.

Rather than everyone gathering in a quiet hotel lobby, the casino and entertainment venue provide a fun and comfortable space for everyone to socialize throughout the weekend.
